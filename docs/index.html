Gift-Wrapped support

Gift-Wrapped lets you make wishlists of links and invite guests with a code. Guests claim items so nobody buys the same gift twice. The list owner never sees who claimed what.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen enter your email and tap "Forgot password?". If an account exists for that email, a reset link is sent.

How do I join a list?

Tap "Join with a code", enter the invite code and your name. Lists you joined appear under "My joined lists" so you do not need the code again.

Can the owner see what I claimed?

No. This is enforced by the database rules, not only by the app. The owner sees how many guests joined and nothing else. Other guests on the same list can see who claimed an item.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in as an owner, open your lists, tap Settings, then Delete account. You enter your password and confirm. Every list you own is deleted, then your account. This cannot be undone.

I joined as a guest and want my name removed.

Guests have no account to delete. "Leave" on the joined-lists screen only removes the list from your device. To have your guest record removed, email us with the list code.

Gift-Wrapped privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10.

Gift-Wrapped is a wishlist app for iOS and Android. This page says what it collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Owners sign up with an email address and a password. They are handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not see your password.
  • Guests give a display name and receive an anonymous Firebase ID. No email or account is needed.
  • List data is stored in Cloud Firestore: list titles, optional dates, invite codes, items (a name, links and a product image address), guest display names, and which guest claimed which item.
  • Product pictures. When an owner saves an item, their device loads the first link's page to find its picture. Anyone viewing the list then loads that picture straight from the shop's servers, which can see the device's IP address, as with any web page.
  • On your device the app keeps your sign-in session and, for guests, a local list of the lists you joined (list id, title and your name).

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking and no analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Who can see what

  • The list owner sees the number of guests who joined. The owner cannot see guest names or claims. The database rules enforce this.
  • Guests on a list can see its items and who claimed each item, by display name.
  • Anyone with an invite code can join the list.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase sends the password reset email when you ask for one.

Deleting your data

  • Owners: in the app, open your lists, tap Settings, then Delete account. This deletes every list you own, with its items and invite code, and then your account.
  • Records that remain: owners cannot read or delete guest and claim records, so deleting an item or a list leaves those records stored. Once a list is deleted they cannot be reached by the app or by any user. Deleting your account does not remove them.
  • Guests: there is no guest account to delete. "Leave" in the app only removes the list from your device. To have your guest record removed, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes.

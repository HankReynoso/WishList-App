Gift-Wrapped is a wishlist where the best part stays a surprise.

Make a list of things you'd love, add a link for each one, and share a short invite code. Your friends and family join with just their name, no account needed. They can see what's on the list and claim the gifts they plan to buy, so nobody ends up with the same present twice.

The twist: you never see what has been claimed. Everyone else knows what's taken. You stay in the dark until the wrapping paper comes off.

How it works
- Create a list for a birthday, a holiday, a wedding, or any occasion, with an optional date and a countdown.
- Add wishes with one or more links. Gift-Wrapped looks up a picture from the first link, so your list looks like a real shop window.
- Share the invite code or link by message, email, or any app.
- Guests join with only a name. They claim items with a tap and can change their mind with Undo.
- Guests see who is getting what, so they can coordinate, for example to split a bigger gift.
- You see how many people joined, and nothing about the claims.

Made with care
- Light and dark appearance that follows your phone.
- Available in English and Spanish.
- No ads, no tracking, and no selling of your data.
- Delete your account and all your lists from inside the app at any time.

Gift-Wrapped keeps the claims hidden from the list owner by design, enforced on the server and not only on screen.

## Review notes for Apple (English)

Gift-Wrapped has two roles. Owners sign in with email and password. Guests join with an invite code and a display name and need no account.

To review the owner side, sign in with the demo account below. It owns a sample list with an invite code.
- Email: DEMO_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER
- Password: DEMO_PASSWORD_PLACEHOLDER

To review the guest side, tap "Join with a code" on the first screen and enter the invite code shown on the sample list, then any name.

The owner can never see which items guests have claimed. This is intentional and is the core of the app.

Account deletion is in Settings, reachable from the person icon on the "My lists" screen.

## App Privacy answers (App Store) and Data safety (Google Play)

Data collected, all linked to the user and used only for app functionality, none for tracking:
- Email address, for owner accounts.
- Name, the display name a guest enters.
- User content, the lists, items, links, and claims.
- Identifiers, an anonymous Firebase ID for guests.

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no data sold or shared with third parties for their own use. Data is processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore).
Users can delete their account and data in the app (owners), and guests have no account to delete.

Age rating: no mature content, so the lowest rating. The app has no user-to-user messaging and no web browsing inside the app, but item links open the device browser.
